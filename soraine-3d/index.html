About

The same stove, pan and egg in four places: a kitchen on Earth, the Moon, Mars and the ISS. Between them, the only thing that changes the physics is gravity.

Each change of world pulses a wave out from the flame. What the wave has passed is already living under the new gravity; what it has not reached is still under the old one. The closer the wavefront is to you, the harder the camera shakes.

Flame shapes here are schematic: height and width follow a simple buoyancy heuristic, not a combustion model. Figures in the detail view are labelled with their source or marked unverified.

Team Soraine, NASA Space Apps 2026. Gravity data, scan wave and loose-prop physics by Tony. The 3D scenes are stylised, not to scale, and were built in code with AI assistance (see the AI log in the project).
